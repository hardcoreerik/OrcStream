Main entry points:

- Eight detailed technical reports in `docs/`.
- Experimental synthetic benchmark scripts, outputs, wire test vectors and validation logs in `experiments/`.
- `FRONTIER_RESEARCH_EXPERIMENTS.md`: eight advanced differentiators and reproducible experiment plans.
- `MAIN_SESSION_INTEGRATION_PROMPT.md`: explicit main-session instructions for integrating findings into canonical OrcStream and the hardware reference project.
- `FOLLOWUP_ACTIONS.md`: prioritized unresolved work.
- `AGENT3_RESEARCH_HANDOFF.md`: original research evidence, baseline and sandbox test methods.
- `manifest.json`: original research source manifest from before the push; retained for reproducibility. The publication SHA and paths are separately documented in `PUSH_PROVENANCE.md` in the handoff archive.

Use the top-level `ROADMAP.md`, `PROJECT_TRUTH.md` and `STATUS.md` for authoritative project state. Research proposals must not override the state ledger. Pluto is a reference platform, not a protocol requirement.
